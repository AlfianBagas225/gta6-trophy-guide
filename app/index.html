Platinum roadmap

Play in stage order. Story stages first — the point-of-no-return flags sit there — then the one-chance moments, then cleanup and the long grinds. Each stage shows its trophies and how many you have earned.

    Point-of-no-return flags

    31 trophies are flagged missable: the 17 story chapter, mission and character-finale trophies, and 14 one-chance moments. Rule of thumb: finish a chapter's flagged trophies before the chapter ends, and do a flagged moment the first time it appears — do not assume a later replay will re-award it. The Missable Watch view lists all 31 in one place.
