      How this guide is built. Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list, as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden). PSN progress targets are used only where PSN lists one — 42 Shooting Range challenges and 500 choices for Actions Speak Louder; every other count comes from the trophy description itself. Each guide is written in our own words from the official description alone: where a description names no place or step, the guide says so instead of inventing one.

      Missable rule. A trophy is flagged missable when it is tied to a specific story mission, chapter or one-off event. Story trophies are flagged because their mission can be passed; free-roam and repeatable trophies are not.

      Works fully offline from its own files. No Rockstar Games artwork or logo is used; the look is our own.
